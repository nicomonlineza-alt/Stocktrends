# Selection Strategy — Draft

## Current status
The dashboard currently uses a fixed illustrative candidate list and placeholder scores. These are for testing the interface only. They are not generated by an AI model and are not based on live or current market data.

## Planned candidate ranking
Once historical and live market data are available, candidate ranking should use measurable features such as:
- Price momentum across multiple windows.
- Relative strength versus a chosen NASDAQ benchmark.
- Volume relative to a recent baseline.
- Volatility and liquidity filters.
- Trend and reversal indicators, with clear definitions.
- Event and earnings-calendar risk flags where reliable data is available.

Feature weights must be selected using training data only, documented, and frozen before evaluation on an unseen period. Avoid using information that would not have been available at the time a prediction is made.

## Daily prediction record
For each daily selection, store:
- Date/time and market timezone.
- Symbol and ranking score.
- Data source and latest quote timestamp.
- Feature values and strategy version.
- Reference price and forecast horizon.
- Later outcome and calculation method.

Never overwrite a prior day's picks after the outcome is known.

## Evaluation
Report prediction accuracy and paper-trading results separately. Include benchmark performance, transaction-cost assumptions, drawdown, number of observations, and results across different market conditions. Compare against simple baselines and evaluate on unseen data before making any performance claim.

## Safety boundary
This is an educational research and paper-trading project. No strategy can guarantee a profit or reliably outthink all market participants. No real brokerage orders are part of this project.
